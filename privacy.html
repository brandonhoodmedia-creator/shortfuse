Privacy Policy — Short Fuse uploader

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The Short Fuse uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Short Fuse YouTube channel to publish the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to upload and manage videos on that YouTube channel (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl). It uses this only to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, privacy settings and playlists, and to read back the status of those uploads.

What the app stores

The app stores the OAuth sign-in token on the channel owner's own computers so it can upload without signing in every time. It does not collect, store or process data about any other person, and it does not read viewers' data, comments or analytics.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Data is sent only to Google's YouTube API to carry out the uploads described above. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it, or deleting the stored token, stops the app from accessing the account.

The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
